# Transit environment kit — credits and modifications

- **FREE // Subway Station & R46 Subway**, Alex Murias / Xlay3D, [source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-subway-station-r46-subway-ae5aadde1c6f48a19b32b309417a669b), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Floors, walls, columns, benches, fixtures, electrical/fire cabinets, track and complete R46 carriage. Changes: architecture cropped into socket-sized modules; train retains complete front/rear geometry and UVs; objects combined by source material; opaque surfaces repaired; textures resized to 2K and metallic/roughness channels repacked for Unity; low wall cutaways. Buffers and reflector details authored for this project.
- **Train Ticket Machine FREE Low Poly**, Lady Lion Studios / LordSamueliSolo, [source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/train-ticket-machine-free-low-poly-9ecb555763a94c408f5f515cbc9e9d5a), CC BY 4.0. Actual kiosk Object_4 only; presentation sphere and its background image excluded. Normalized to 1.85 m height and converted to FBX/PBR textures.
- **power_box_01** and **old_military_compressor**, [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/models), CC0. Source PBR preserved, metallic/roughness repacked, converted to FBX. Individual download provenance retained in the local donor folders.

Fictional Nexus signage and placement/lighting code are project work. No paid assets, historical Japanese ticket scan, or entire source scene is distributed here.
