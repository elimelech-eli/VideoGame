The original OGG files are renamed, otherwise unmodified. Unity streams and loops
them at a restrained volume, with a 1.5-second zone crossfade. M or the HUD music
button mutes/unmutes both channels. These are licensed music recordings, not the
procedural pulse/blast sound effects used by the combat prototype.
