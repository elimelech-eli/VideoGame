Selected models: crawler, roller, blaster, lobber. Distinct original meshes and rigs.
Exports preserve original idle, move, attack and die1 actions. Unity clips alias
these actions to the gameplay animation states; playback speed adapts attack duration.
Textures are included as published; Unity materials tint the colorable panel map.
Reproduce FBX exports using Tools/Art/export_robots.py and Blender 4.5 with the
original archive extracted locally. Blender is not required to build or play.
