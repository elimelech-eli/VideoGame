# Art checkpoint

The approved runtime Operator v03, its editable master/bound Blender files,
reference images, tooling, licenses and review records are versioned here.
Review images, manifests and scripts preserve the history of rejected drafts.

Large intermediate Blender scenes, downloaded/generated raw meshes, repeated
PNG renders and backup files remain on this development machine and are ignored.
This avoids publishing restricted experimental generation outputs and multi-GB
intermediates (some exceed GitHub's 100 MB per-file limit). They have not been
deleted. Rejected studies are not installed as runtime assets.

On 2026-10-28 the six environment studies and 24 static enemy role assemblies
were rejected: architecture did not meet the desired quality, and role variants
shared too much body geometry. Professional free environment kits are the next
source direction. These drafts are reference history, not approved production art.
